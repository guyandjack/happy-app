Showcase site

What is a showcase site ?

A showcase site is a website that presents your company, your services and your achievements. It clearly indicates to visitors your activity, your contact details. It constitutes a professional online presence, accessible at any time.

Why create a showcase site ?

A professional showcase site allows you to strengthen your credibility, attract new customers and highlights your knowledge do and areas of expertise. It facilitates making contact, quote requests and appointments.

Retail site

What is an e-commerce site ?

An e-commerce siteis an online sales space, open 24 hours a day, it is designed to offer a simple, intuitive purchasing journey, from product discovery to payment. Its interface adapts to mobiles and computers to facilitate navigation, item selection and order finalization.

Why create an e-commerce site ?

An e-commerce site allows you to adapt the functionalities to your activity: product catalog, payment secure, inventory management, delivery or in-store collection. It simplifies daily management, improves customer experience and supports the development of your business.

Customized site

What is a custom website ?

A tailor-made website is designed to meet the specific needs of a business, beyond functionalities of a classic site (showcase or e-commerce). It can integrate tools profession, custom features or connections to existing software.

Why create a tailor-made website ?

A tailor-made site is particularly suitable when existing solutions do not meet the needs. needs of the company. It allows to develop specific functionalities, to simplify internal processes and to have a scalable tool, adapted to its organization.

A concrete example

A property maintenance company can have a single portal allowing customers to report a breakdown, technicians to follow their interventions, managers to manage the priorities and salespeople to present the properties for rent. A centralized tool, designed around needs real of company.

Frequently asked questions

  • The cost of creating a website depends on its complexity, features desired and design. A simple showcase site starts from CHF 1’500, (4 to 6 pages) while an e-commerce site or application web custom represents a larger investment.   • The number of pages depends mainly on your objectives, your activity and your strategy digital.
    For a classic showcase site, 5 to 6 pages are generally recommended: home, services, about, contact, legal notices and confidentiality policy. This structure allows you to clearly organize content and optimize each page for keywords specific.
    There are also one-page sites, where all sections (presentation, services, testimonials, contact, etc.) are grouped together on a single page. This format is particularly suitable for activity launches, campaigns marketing or simple projects.

    From an SEO point of view, a single page site can be effective for a main query, but it remains more limited to position yourself on several keywords or on local searches varied. A site structured into several pages generally offers better SEO performance, because each page can target a specific search intent.
    The choice between a single-page site and a multi-page site therefore depends on your strategy: maximum visibility on Google, lead generation, or simple online presence.

  • The lead time mainly depends on the complexity of the project, features requested and the desired level of customization.
    On average, a professional showcase site requires 4 to 6 weeks, including the strategy, design, development, SEO optimization and testing phase.
    An e-commerce site generally requires 8 to 12 weeks, due to integration of payments, product management, logistics and security aspects.
    A one-page site, especially when designed with web-assisted tools artificial intelligence, can be achieved much faster: a few days to 2 weeks depending on the level of customization and SEO optimization.
    Please note: after purchasing a domain name, a technical DNS propagation delay (usually from 4 to 24 hours) may be required before the site is accessible everywhere.
    Each project being unique, a detailed and realistic planning is always established at launch to ensure smooth and controlled development.
