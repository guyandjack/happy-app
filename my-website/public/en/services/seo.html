SEO Audit

What is an SEO audit?

A SEO audit is an analysis of your website which makes it possible to identify problems that could slow down its natural referencing. It examines in particular the indexing, the structure of the site, technical performance, content, internal links and local visibility.

Why do an SEO audit?

An SEO audit allows you to understand the points to improve in order to increase the visibility of your site on search engines. It leads to concrete and hierarchical recommendations, in order to correct the identified problems and concentrate efforts on the most relevant actions.

Technical SEO

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO consists of optimizing the structure and the functioning of a site to facilitate its exploration, its understanding and indexing by search engines. In particular, it checks the semantic structure of the pages(HTML tags), URLs, redirects, sitemap, the robots.txt file and structured data.

Why optimize technical SEO?

A consistent semantic HTML structure constitutes an essential basis for natural referencing. Without this basis, certain optimizations risk losing in efficiency. Technical SEO also aims to improve loading speed, mobile experience and Core Web Vitals, to facilitate access to contents and their use.

SEO Off-page

What is off-page SEO?

The SEO off-page groups together the actions carried out outside your site to strengthen its notoriety and credibility. It concerns in particular links coming from other sites (backlinks), mentions of your company and your presence on external platforms.

Why optimize off-page SEO?

A credible online presence and links from relevant sites can build trust search engines towards your site. Backlink analysis and development quality links help to improve your authority and visibility in search results.

GEO: optimization for generative engines

What is GEO?

The GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) consists of to optimize the contents of a site to facilitate their understanding and their use by search engines and AI assistants, like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI Overviews. It complements traditional SEO by favoring information clear, reliable and verifiable.

Why optimize the GEO of your site?

The GEOaims to increase the chances that your company and your content are mentioned or cited in the AI-generated responses. It is based on precise answers, a clear semantic structure, verifiable information and structured data schema.org relevant, without guaranteeing their recovery by AI assistants.
