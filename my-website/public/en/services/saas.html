SaaS Software

What is SaaS ?

A SaaS (Software as a Service) is software accessible online, without installation on users' devices. It provides access to a secure workspace from a browser, with centralized data and regular updates.

Why create a SaaS ?

The development of a SaaS allows us to offer business software accessible to several users or companies. This model facilitates centralized data management, maintenance and evolution of functionalities. It also allows you to market a service in the form of monthly or annual subscription..

How a SaaS works

How a SaaS works ?

A SaaSworks from one platform hosted online, accessible from a browser. It is based on three elements: a user interface, a backend which manages the functionalities and a database that stores information. Each user has access adapted to their role.

Depending on the needs, a SaaS can integrate the subscription management, payments, notifications, dashboards or connections to other software via API. Updates are centralized and accessible to users without installation.

Concrete use cases

A concrete example of SaaS: Wizpix

Wizpix.ch is a Swiss SaaS that offers online image processing tools. In particular, it allows you to remove the background of an image and convert files in different formats, directly from a browser.

The platform is intended to evolve with new features, including video creation in motion design to present products or promote brands. An example of scalable SaaS software, accessible without installation.

Software designed to grow with your business

A tailor-made SaaS must be able to adapt as your needs evolve: new users, additional features or connection to other tools. Its architecture is designed from departure to facilitate these developments.

Development favors a reliable and maintainable technical base, with particular attention to safety data and access management. The goal is to have durable software, without unnecessary complexity.
