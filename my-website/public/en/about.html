My charter ethics

I am committed to developing sites and apps who respect both
the environment and good practices digital.
I ensure that every aspect of my work is as close as possible to Green IT recommendations.

  • Customer satisfaction 1

    Putting the user at the center, measuring satisfaction and continuously improving experience.

  • Transparency 2

    Work with honesty, clearly explain technical choices and propose solutions adapted.

  • Respect for privacy 3

    Protect users' personal data by respecting regulations (GDPR, nLPD).

  • Eco-design 4

    Reducing environmental impact by optimizing resources, media and performance.

  • Accessibility 5

    Design inclusive interfaces, accessible to people with disabilities, whatever or their device.

  • Sustainability 6

    Develop scalable projects, easy to maintain, designed to last over time and limit unnecessary recasts.

Because digital can (and must) be more respectful,
each project is for me an opportunity to contribute
to a more sustainable and more human web.

My charter eco‑responsible

eco‑web designconsists of reduce the environmental impact of a website from its design, without compromising its usefulness. This approach is based on five essential principles.

  • Focus on what matters: favor truly useful features and simplify user journeys.

  • Develop a lightweight site: choose a suitable architecture and limit unnecessary scripts, libraries and processing.

  • Reduce transferred data: optimize images, videos and files, and use caching to avoid repeated downloads.

  • Fostering sustainability: design sites compatible with existing devices and browsers for limit the renewal of equipment.

  • Choose responsible hosting: energy-efficient infrastructure, and powered by electricity with a low environmental impact.

These principles are based in particular on the ecodesign framework web RWEB from GreenIT. Their application depends on the needs and constraints of each project.

My sites are hosted at PlanetHoster, a host who communicates about its commitments environmental and energy efficiency of its infrastructure.
